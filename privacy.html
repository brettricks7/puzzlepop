Privacy Policy — Puzzle Pop

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version

Puzzle Pop is a single-player pastel puzzle game. Progress, coins, cosmetics, and music choices stay on your device. Optional first-party analytics only run when configured, and ads / real-money purchases stay gated behind an adult challenge.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress (level, coins, piggy bank, cosmetics, music, difficulty start) in local app storage.
  • A random install UUID for first-party analytics when enabled — not an advertising ID, Android ID, or hardware identifier.

What we may collect (when analytics is on)

When first-party analytics is configured, we may receive a random install UUID, session id, app version, age band (not a birthdate), and gameplay events such as level start/complete/timeout, Done presses, hint use, and coin earn/spend. We do not collect AAID, precise location, phone number, email, birthdate, or free-text names. Share grids stay on the clipboard; we do not post them.

Ads and purchases

Release builds use Google test ad unit IDs only until a deliberate ads SDK wiring. Real-money SKUs are placeholders. Child-directed / unknown age bands refuse personalized ads; adult verification is required for money flows.

Internet

Without a configured analytics ingest URL, the game does not send gameplay events. League / ingest servers are not deployed with this client build.

Children

Puzzle Pop is designed as a family-friendly puzzle. Analytics mode stays kids-safe until adult verification. Parents can reset the analytics id from the home screen via a multi-step parent gate.
